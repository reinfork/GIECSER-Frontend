# DESIGN.md — GIECSER ASR

School speaking-practice tool. English-only UI (Indonesian proper nouns —
Jong Layo, Melayu Riau — stay as content, never translated).

- Palette, in combination: soft white/black theme base, violet action color,
  light khaki (`#EAE3CC`) surfaces. Violet marks the action moment (CTAs,
  active states, the AI highlight). Khaki carries calm surfaces.
- Landing: dark-grey dotted band, one violet-to-khaki gradient hero panel
  (owner direction; R-01 recorded override, landing only).
- Style: minimalist editorial case-study (numbered sections, generous
  whitespace) + soft neumorphism (low-contrast raised khaki, no glow
  stacking). Photos: stock classroom shots from public/ only.
- Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. One focal point per screen (landing:
  the class-code form).
- Hard lines: light/dark toggle must both work (neumorphism flattens to dark
  slate in dark mode rather than faking depth); phone layout stacks; no
  invented numbers, names, testimonials, or claims — copy comes from the
  e-book (Riau Malay coastal wisdom, Grade VIII, CEFR A2, Kurikulum Merdeka)
  or it does not ship. Student PIN entry and teacher login never merge.
